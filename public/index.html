How it goes

Our 7-step install

Every project is a little different, but a professional install follows this order.

Ask about prep When you compare quotes, don't just compare coatings. A premium coating can't make up for a poorly prepared slab.
1

Floor evaluation

We check the slab: old coatings, cracks, damage, contamination and moisture. We also talk about how you use the room.

2

Choose your system

Colors, flake blends, textures and coating types, so you pick a system that fits the space and your budget.

3

Prepare the concrete

Mechanical diamond grinding removes contaminants and weak material and creates the profile the coating needs to bond.

4

Repair imperfections

Cracks, pits and spalls get filled. Concrete moves, so repairs can't promise cracks never return, but they give a better base.

5

Apply the system

Primer, base coat and decorative material go down in order. On flake floors, the chips are broadcast into the wet base coat.

6

Protective topcoat

A clear topcoat locks it in and adds abrasion resistance, chemical resistance, gloss and texture.

7

Proper cure time

We tell you exactly when you can walk on it, move things back and park on it again.

Questions

Epoxy flooring FAQ

Straight answers to what Queen Creek homeowners and businesses ask us most.

How long does epoxy flooring last?+
There's no single answer. It depends on the system, prep, install quality, traffic, environment and maintenance. A home garage and a busy warehouse put very different demands on a floor.
Can you coat cracked concrete?+
Most cracks and damaged spots can be repaired before coating. Concrete can keep moving, though, so future movement may show through. We look at existing damage during prep.
Can epoxy go over an old coating?+
Sometimes. We check the existing coating's condition, adhesion and compatibility. Often it needs to be ground off or abraded before the new system goes down.
Will it hold up to the Arizona sun?+
Standard epoxy can yellow under UV. For patios and garages that see direct sun, we use UV-stable polyaspartic topcoats chosen for heat, sunlight and monsoon moisture.
Is epoxy flooring slippery?+
Any smooth floor can be slippery when wet. We can add texture or aggregate for traction. More texture means better grip, while smoother floors are easier to mop, so we help you find the right balance.
Can I pick my own colors?+
Yes. Depending on the system, there are many solid colors and flake blends to choose from, plus metallic finishes.
Is it easy to clean?+
Yes. Regular sweeping removes grit, and a mop with a suitable cleaner handles the rest. Wipe up spills promptly and avoid dragging sharp or very heavy items.
How much does epoxy flooring cost in Queen Creek?+
Price depends on square footage, slab condition, prep and repairs, the system, decorative options and number of coats. The best way to get an accurate number is a free on-site estimate.
