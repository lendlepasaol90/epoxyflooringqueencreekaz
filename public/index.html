Queen Creek's epoxy flooring crew

Concrete is strong, but bare concrete makes a poor finished floor. It soaks up oil and stains, sheds dust and leaves a garage or shop looking unfinished. Out here it also bakes in the sun and collects desert grit.

At Epoxy Flooring Queen Creek AZ, we install coating systems for homes and businesses across Queen Creek and nearby communities. A floor that lasts comes from checking the concrete, grinding it right, repairing cracks, choosing the right system and letting it cure properly.

Why epoxy

Why choose epoxy flooring?

Resinous coatings go directly over prepared concrete, with no seams or grout lines. That's why they're used everywhere from home garages to industrial plants.

01

Attractive look

A forgotten garage starts to feel like part of the home. Shops look brighter and more professional.

02

Durable surface

Hard surfaces that stand up to foot traffic, vehicles, tools and equipment.

03

Easy cleaning

Dust, dirt and spills sit on top instead of soaking into porous concrete.

04

Your design

Flake blends, solid colors, metallics and textures to match your space.

05

Seamless

One continuous surface from wall to wall. No grout lines to scrub.

How it goes

Our 7-step install

Every project is a little different, but a professional install follows this order.

01

Floor evaluation

We check the slab for old coatings, cracks, damage, contamination and moisture, and talk about how you use the room.

02

Choose your system

Colors, flake blends, textures and coating types that fit the space and your budget.

03

Prepare the concrete

Diamond grinding removes contaminants and weak material and creates the profile the coating needs to bond.

04

Repair imperfections

Cracks, pits and spalls get filled. Concrete moves, so cracks can return, but repairs give a better base.

05

Apply the system

Primer, base coat and decorative material go down in order. Flakes are broadcast into the wet base coat.

06

Protective topcoat

A clear topcoat adds abrasion and chemical resistance, gloss and texture.

07

Proper cure time

We tell you when you can walk on it, move things back and park on it again.

!

Ask about prep

When comparing quotes, don't just compare coatings. A premium coating can't make up for a poorly prepared slab.
